How to scan

  1. On the shop screen, open the job. It shows at the top of this page.
  2. Tap the green Start camera button. Allow camera access if the phone asks.
  3. Hold the phone 4–8 inches from the barcode, bars running up and down inside the white box.
  4. A beep and a green flash mean it worked: the part is on the job, here and on the shop screen.
  5. "Not in the parts list" means it's new: type what it is and the price, and it's saved to the parts list and added to the job.
  6. Won't read? Move back a little, turn on the light, or tap Type barcode.
